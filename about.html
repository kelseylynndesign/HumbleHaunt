Hello There!

I’m Kelsey Kendrick, the creator of Humble Haunt and the resident Tarot Reader. I first bought a classic Rider Waite Smith deck from a hippie shop in interior B.C. as a teenager on summer vacation and I’ve been reading ever since.

As a reader, I’m known for my intuition, empathy, and playful nature. Fortune Telling doesn’t need to be serious!

I am happy to read on any topic, but health readings do hold a special place in my heart. As a witch with chronic illness struggles I love being able to help other’s navigate their medical journeys.

I’ve always been a collector of decks, drawn to study the different interpretations and themes that each creator brings to the legacy of this tradition. It was this desire to put my own spin on Tarot that led me to start my work on the Humble Haunt deck.
